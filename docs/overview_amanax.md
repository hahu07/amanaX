## AmanaX Overview

AmanaX is an AI-powered Islamic capital market infrastructure platform built to modernise the Nigerian capital market. It enables fund managers, issuing houses, trustees, custodians, Shariah advisors, distributors, investors, and regulators to collaboratively design, structure, approve, issue, distribute, and manage Shariah-compliant and asset-backed investment products through a secure digital platform.

The platform is designed to support a broad range of Islamic capital market instruments, including investment notes, Sukuk, Ijara funds, Musharakah funds, Mudarabah funds, Murabahah-backed investment products, trade finance funds, and other Shariah-compliant investment structures. For the MVP, AmanaX demonstrates the digital issuance and lifecycle management of a **Shariah-compliant Investment Note**, showcasing how regulated institutions can collaborate through secure digital workflows. The same platform can later support many other Islamic capital market instruments without changing its core architecture.

Today, issuing and managing investment products in Nigeria often requires multiple independent institutions to coordinate through disconnected systems, emails, spreadsheets, and paper-based processes. Managing approvals between fund managers, issuing houses, trustees, Shariah advisors, custodians, and the Securities and Exchange Commission is often slow, costly, and difficult to monitor. These fragmented processes delay product launches, increase operational risk, and make regulatory and Shariah compliance more complex.

AmanaX digitises the entire investment product lifecycle—from product design and structuring to approvals, regulatory filing, issuance, investor onboarding, subscription management, servicing, profit distribution, and regulatory reporting. AI agents assist participants by recommending product structures, reviewing compliance requirements, assessing risks, preparing documentation, monitoring product performance, and generating reports, while all investment, regulatory, and Shariah decisions remain with authorised professionals.

Built on the Canton Network, AmanaX provides privacy-preserving collaboration, secure multi-party workflows, and a shared source of truth for all participants. By modernising how Shariah-compliant investment products are created and managed, AmanaX helps strengthen Nigeria's Islamic capital market, reduce operational costs, improve transparency, accelerate product issuance, and increase investor confidence.
